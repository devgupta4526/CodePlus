# 13. Simple & Compound Interest

---

## 1. Interest Formulae & Difference Shortcuts

- **Simple Interest (SI)**: $\text{SI} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$
- **2-Year CI vs SI Difference Shortcut**:
  $$\text{CI}_2 - \text{SI}_2 = P \left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2$$
