# 10. Ratio, Proportion & Partnership

---

## 1. Ratio & Proportion Rules

- **Proportion**: $\frac{A}{B} = \frac{C}{D} \implies A \times D = B \times C$.
- **Mean Proportional** between $A$ and $B$: $\sqrt{A \times B}$.
- **Third Proportional** to $A$ and $B$: $\frac{B^2}{A}$.
- **Fourth Proportional** to $A, B, C$: $\frac{B \times C}{A}$.

---

## 2. Commercial Partnership Mathematics

When business partners invest initial financial capital $C$ over time duration $T$, annual generated profit $P$ is divided in direct ratio of their **Capital $	imes$ Time** products:
$$\text{Profit Ratio }(P_1 : P_2) = (C_1 \times T_1) : (C_2 \times T_2)$$
