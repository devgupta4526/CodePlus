# 15. Blood Relations

---

## 1. What are Blood Relations?

Blood relation problems test your ability to trace familial lineages and map out complex relationships using provided clues. 

---

## 2. Standard Family Tree Symbols

To solve complex questions without confusion, always draw a **Family Tree Diagram** using standard symbolic notations:

### Gender Symbols
- **Male**: Represented by a Box $\square$ or a Plus sign $(+)$.
- **Female**: Represented by a Circle $\bigcirc$ or a Minus sign $(-)$.

### Relationship Connecting Lines
- **Horizontal Line ($-$)**: Connects siblings (Brother/Sister).
- **Double Horizontal Line ($=$)**: Connects a married couple (Husband/Wife).
- **Vertical Line ($|$)**: Connects different generations (Parent to Child).

---

## 3. Generational Tiers

- **Generation +2**: Grandfather, Grandmother (Maternal/Paternal)
- **Generation +1**: Father, Mother, Uncle, Aunt, Father-in-law, Mother-in-law
- **Generation 0**: You, Brother, Sister, Cousin, Husband, Wife, Brother-in-law, Sister-in-law
- **Generation -1**: Son, Daughter, Nephew, Niece, Son-in-law, Daughter-in-law
- **Generation -2**: Grandson, Granddaughter

---

## 4. Key Terminology (The Traps)

1. **Maternal vs Paternal**: 
   - *Maternal* = Mother's side of the family (Maternal Uncle = Mother's brother).
   - *Paternal* = Father's side of the family.
2. **Nephew & Niece**:
   - *Nephew* = Brother's or Sister's Son (Male).
   - *Niece* = Brother's or Sister's Daughter (Female).
3. **Only Son / Only Daughter**:
   - If A is the *only son* of B, it means B has no other male children. B could still have 5 daughters.
   - If A is the *only child* of B, then A has absolutely no siblings.
4. **"He is the son of the only son of my grandfather"**: Break it down backwards. The only son of my grandfather is my Father. Therefore, he is the son of my Father. Hence, he is my Brother (or me, if I am male).
