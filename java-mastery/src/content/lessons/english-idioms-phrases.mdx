# Idioms and Phrases

> [!NOTE]
> An **Idiom** is a phrase whose meaning cannot be understood from the literal definitions of the individual words.

## Top 10 High-Frequency Idioms

1. **A blessing in disguise**  
   *Meaning:* A good thing that seemed bad at first.  
   *Example:* Losing that job turned out to be a blessing in disguise because it pushed me to start my own business.

2. **Bite the bullet**  
   *Meaning:* To force yourself to perform a difficult or unpleasant task.  
   *Example:* I had to bite the bullet and tell my boss the truth.

3. **Break a leg**  
   *Meaning:* Good luck! (Used mostly in theater before a performance).  
   *Example:* Break a leg on your exam tomorrow!

4. **Burn the midnight oil**  
   *Meaning:* To read, study, or work late into the night.  
   *Example:* She's been burning the midnight oil to finish her project.

5. **Cut corners**  
   *Meaning:* To do something poorly in order to save time or money.  
   *Example:* The builders cut corners, and now the roof is leaking.

6. **Hit the nail on the head**  
   *Meaning:* To describe exactly what is causing a situation or problem.  
   *Example:* You hit the nail on the head when you said we need more communication.

7. **Let the cat out of the bag**  
   *Meaning:* To reveal a secret carelessly or by mistake.  
   *Example:* We were planning a surprise party, but my brother let the cat out of the bag.

8. **Once in a blue moon**  
   *Meaning:* Something that happens very rarely.  
   *Example:* I only eat junk food once in a blue moon.

9. **Piece of cake**  
   *Meaning:* A task that is very easy to accomplish.  
   *Example:* That math test was a piece of cake.

10. **Spill the beans**  
    *Meaning:* To disclose a secret.  
    *Example:* Come on, spill the beans! What did she say?

> [!TIP]
> Try to visualize the idiom! Imagining someone literally "letting a cat out of a bag" helps cement the meaning of revealing a hidden secret.
