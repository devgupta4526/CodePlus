## 1. Mean, Median, Mode & Standard Deviation

- **Karl Pearson's Empirical Formula**: $\text{Mode} = 3(\text{Median}) - 2(\text{Mean})$.
- **Variance ($\sigma^2$)**: Average squared deviation from arithmetic mean. $\text{Standard Deviation} = \sqrt{\text{Variance}}$.
- *Note: Adding or subtracting a constant shift to all observations alters Mean by that exact constant, but leaves Standard Deviation completely unchanged!*
