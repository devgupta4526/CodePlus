# 7. Algebra & Polynomials

---

## 1. Quadratic Equations & Roots

For second-degree polynomial $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$:
- **Sum of Roots**: $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$, **Product of Roots**: $\alpha \beta = \frac{c}{a}$.
- **Discriminant**: $D = b^2 - 4ac$. If $D=0$, roots are real and equal!

---

## 2. Reciprocal Powers Shortcuts

If given $x + \frac{1}{x} = k$:
1. $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = k^2 - 2$
2. $x^3 + \frac{1}{x^3} = k^3 - 3k$
