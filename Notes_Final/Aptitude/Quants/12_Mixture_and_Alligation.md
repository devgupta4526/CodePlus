# 12. Mixture & Alligation

## 1. The Alligation Cross Rule

When combining a cheaper ingredient (cost $C$) and a dearer ingredient (cost $D$) to create a blended mean strength (price $M$):
$$\frac{\text{Quantity of Cheaper}}{\text{Quantity of Dearer}} = \frac{D - M}{M - C}$$
