# 11. Profit, Loss & Discount

---

## 1. Commercial Pricing Rules

- **Profit %**: $\frac{\text{SP} - \text{CP}}{\text{CP}} \times 100$ (Always calculated over Cost Price).
- **Discount %**: $\frac{\text{MP} - \text{SP}}{\text{MP}} \times 100$ (Always calculated over Marked Price).
- **Direct CP to MP Ratio Shortcut**:
  $$\frac{\text{MP}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{100 + \text{Profit } \%}{100 - \text{Discount } \%}$$
