# 14. Time, Work & Pipes & Cisterns

---

## 1. LCM Unit Work Method & MDH Rule

- **LCM Total Work Method**: Total Work = LCM of individual time taken. Daily efficiency $E = \text{Total Work} / \text{Days}$.
- **Pipes Rule**: Inlet filling pipes have $+E$, draining outlet pipes have $-E$.
- **MDH Chain Formula**: $\frac{M_1 \times D_1 \times H_1}{W_1} = \frac{M_2 \times D_2 \times H_2}{W_2}$.
