## 1. Probability Rules & Sample Spaces

- **Probability Definition**: $P(E) = \frac{\text{Favorable Outcomes}}{\text{Total Sample Space Size}}$.
- **Two Dice Sample Space**: Total combinations $= 6^2 = 36$ pairs.
- **Standard Card Deck**: 52 cards (26 Red, 26 Black; 12 total portrait face cards).
