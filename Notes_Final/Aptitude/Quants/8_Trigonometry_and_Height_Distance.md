# 8. Trigonometry & Height-Distance

## 1. Pythagorean Identities & Complementary Angles

- $\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1$
- $\sec^2 \theta - \tan^2 \theta = 1 \implies$ If $\sec \theta + \tan \theta = k$, then $\sec \theta - \tan \theta = \frac{1}{k}$.
- **Complementary**: $\tan A \times \tan B = 1$ when $A + B = 90^\circ$.

---

## 2. Heights & Distances Standard Triangles

- In $30^\circ - 60^\circ - 90^\circ$ triangles, edge lengths scale in exact ratio **$1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$**.
