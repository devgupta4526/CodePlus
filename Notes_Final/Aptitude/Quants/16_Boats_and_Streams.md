## 1. Downstream & Upstream Velocity Rules

Let still water boat speed be $B$ and flowing current velocity be $S$:
- **Downstream Speed ($D$)**: $D = B + S$.
- **Upstream Speed ($U$)**: $U = B - S$.
- **Still Water Boat Speed ($B$)**: $B = \frac{D + U}{2}$.
- **Stream Current Velocity ($S$)**: $S = \frac{D - U}{2}$.
