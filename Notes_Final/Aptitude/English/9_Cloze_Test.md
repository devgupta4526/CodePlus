# Cloze Test

> [!TIP]
> A **Cloze Test** is a passage with some words removed (blanks). You must select the correct word for each blank to make the passage meaningful and grammatically correct.

## Effective Strategies

### 1. Read the Whole Passage First
Never start filling blanks immediately. Read the entire passage with the blanks to grasp the context, tone, and tense of the story. 

### 2. Check the Grammar
Use grammar rules to eliminate wrong options. 
* If the blank is after an article like 'an', the answer must start with a vowel sound.
* If the blank is after 'to', it's usually the base form of a verb (V1).
* If the sentence is in past tense, eliminate present tense verb options.

### 3. Look Forward and Backward
The clue for a blank is often in the sentence immediately before or after it. Look at the surrounding preposition.
* Example: He is very fond ____ playing cricket. (The word 'fond' is always followed by 'of').

### 4. Tone of the Paragraph
If the paragraph is discussing a disaster, look for negative words. If it's about an achievement, look for positive words.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Elimination is your best friend.** Cross out options that don't fit grammatically, then cross out options that don't fit the tone. You will usually be left with the correct answer.
