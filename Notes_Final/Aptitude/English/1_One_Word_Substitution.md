# One Word Substitution

> [!TIP]
> **One Word Substitution** simplifies sentences by replacing a group of words with a single word. This is crucial for clear and concise writing, and heavily tested in competitive exams.

## Highly Repeated Words

Here is a curated list of high-frequency one-word substitutions from past exams:

| Phrase | One Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A person who does not believe in God | **Atheist** | नास्तिक |
| A person who looks at the bright side of things | **Optimist** | आशावादी |
| A person who looks at the dark side of things | **Pessimist** | निराशावादी |
| A life history written by oneself | **Autobiography** | आत्मकथा |
| A life history written by someone else | **Biography** | जीवनी |
| A place where birds are kept | **Aviary** | पक्षीशाला |
| A place where bees are kept | **Apiary** | मधुमक्खी-शाला |
| One who knows everything | **Omniscient** | सर्वज्ञ |
| One who is all-powerful | **Omnipotent** | सर्वशक्तिमान |
| One who is present everywhere | **Omnipresent** | सर्वव्यापी |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Memory Trick:** Words ending in **-cide** often mean "killing" (e.g., *Suicide* - self, *Homicide* - human, *Patricide* - father). Words starting with **omni-** mean "all".
